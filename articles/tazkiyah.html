Tazkiyah al-Nafs | Purification of the Soul

Foundations of Faith 15 Min Read By Noor Al-Huda Team

Tazkiyah refers to the purification of the soul (Nafs). It is the process of cleansing the heart from spiritual diseases like arrogance, envy, and greed, and adorning it with noble traits like patience, gratitude, and sincerity. It is the journey from the lower self to the tranquil soul.

"He has succeeded who purifies it, And he has failed who instills it [with corruption]." Surah Ash-Shams, 91:9-10

Why Tazkiyah Matters

In Islam, faith is not just about external actions; it is deeply rooted in the state of the heart. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: "Verily, in the body there is a piece of flesh which, if it is sound, the whole body is sound, and if it is corrupt, the whole body is corrupt. Verily, it is the heart." (Bukhari & Muslim). Tazkiyah ensures this "piece of flesh" remains healthy and connected to Allah.

The Three Stages of the Soul

Scholars describe the soul's journey in three stages:

  • An-Nafs Al-Ammarah: The soul that commands evil (driven by desires).
  • An-Nafs Al-Lawwamah: The self-reproaching soul (conscious of sins and seeks repentance).
  • An-Nafs Al-Mutma'innah: The tranquil soul (at peace with Allah's decree).

Common Spiritual Diseases

Just as the body gets sick, the heart can fall ill. Recognizing these diseases is the first step toward cure:

Arrogance (Kibr)

Believing oneself superior to others. It was the sin of Iblis and prevents one from entering Paradise.

Envy (Hasad)

Resenting the blessings Allah has given to others. It consumes good deeds like fire consumes wood.

Love of World (Hubb al-Dunya)

Allowing worldly pursuits to distract from the remembrance of Allah and the Hereafter.

Showing Off (Riya)

Performing acts of worship to be seen by people rather than for the sake of Allah.

The Path to Purification

Tazkiyah is not a passive process; it requires active effort (Mujahadah). Here are key steps to cleanse the heart:

  • Knowledge (Ilm)

    Learning about Allah's names and attributes increases love and awe, pushing out love for lesser things.

  • Worship (Ibadah)

    Regular prayer, fasting, and Quran recitation polish the heart and remove the rust of heedlessness.

  • Remembrance (Dhikr)

    Constantly remembering Allah brings tranquility. "Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts find rest." (13:28)

  • Good Company

    Sitting with righteous people reminds you of Allah and encourages good behavior.

The Role of Repentance (Tawbah)

No matter how hard we try, we will make mistakes. Tawbah is the tool that resets our spiritual state. It is not just saying "sorry," but feeling remorse, stopping the sin, and resolving not to return to it. Allah loves those who constantly turn to Him in repentance.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "By Him in Whose Hand is my life, if you were not to commit sin, Allah would sweep you out of existence and He would replace (you) by those people who would commit sin and seek forgiveness from Allah..."

— Sahih Muslim

Conclusion

Tazkiyah is a lifelong journey. It requires patience, consistency, and reliance on Allah. By cleansing our hearts, we attain Sakinah (tranquility) in this life and prepare ourselves for the ultimate success in the Hereafter.

"And whoever fears Allah - He will make for him a way out And will provide for him from where he does not expect." Surah At-Talaq, 65:2-3
