The 99 Names of Allah

Foundations of Faith Reference Guide By Noor Al-Huda Team

Allah has revealed Himself to humanity through His beautiful names and attributes. Known as Al-Asma ul-Husna, these names provide a comprehensive understanding of who Allah is. Below is the complete list with Arabic script, transliteration, and detailed reflections.

"And to Allah belong the best names, so invoke Him by them..." Surah Al-A'raf, 7:180

How to Use These Names

Don't just read them—invoke them. If you are anxious, call upon As-Salam (The Source of Peace). If you are in need, call upon Ar-Razzaq (The Provider). Let these names transform your Dua from a request into a conversation with your Creator.

The Complete List

Reflect on each name, its meaning, and its manifestation in your daily life.

1 Mercy

الرَّحْمَنُ

Ar-Rahman

The Most Gracious

His mercy encompasses all creation in this world, believer and non-believer alike. It is an intense, immediate mercy that sustains existence.

2 Mercy

الرَّحِيمُ

Ar-Rahim

The Most Merciful

His specific mercy reserved for the believers in the Hereafter. It is a permanent, enduring mercy that leads to eternal peace.

3 Power

الْمَلِكُ

Al-Malik

The King

The Absolute Ruler who owns everything in the heavens and the earth. Unlike human kings, His sovereignty is perfect and never declines.

4 Perfection

الْقُدُّوسُ

Al-Quddus

The Holy

The One who is pure from any imperfection, error, or negligence. He is far above anything that does not befit His majesty.

5 Peace

السَّلَامُ

As-Salam

The Source of Peace

The One who is free from every defect and who grants peace and security to His creation. True peace is found only in remembering Him.

6 Security

الْمُؤْمِنُ

Al-Mu'min

The Guardian of Faith

The One who gives security and safety to His creation. He is faithful to His promises and protects the believers from fear.

7 Protection

الْمُهَيْمِنُ

Al-Muhaymin

The Protector

The Witness who watches over everything. He preserves and maintains the existence of all things, knowing every detail.

8 Power

الْعَزِيزُ

Al-Aziz

The Almighty

The Strong and Defeater who is never defeated. True honor and power come only from Him, not from worldly status.

9 Power

الْجَبَّارُ

Al-Jabbar

The Compeller

The One who restores broken things and compels things to be as He wills. He mends the hearts of the broken-hearted.

10 Majesty

الْمُتَكَبِّرُ

Al-Mutakabbir

The Supreme

The One who is greater than everything else. Greatness belongs to Him alone, and arrogance is forbidden for humans.

11 Creation

الْخَالِقُ

Al-Khaliq

The Creator

The One who brings everything into existence from non-existence. He creates with perfect measure and wisdom.

12 Creation

الْبَارِئُ

Al-Bari'

The Evolver

The Maker who creates living beings with distinct characteristics. He evolves creation from one stage to another perfectly.

13 Creation

الْمُصَوِّرُ

Al-Musawwir

The Fashioner

The Shaper who gives each creation its unique form and appearance. No two faces or fingerprints are alike.

14 Forgiveness

الْغَفَّارُ

Al-Ghaffar

The All-Forgiving

The One who forgives repeatedly. No matter how many times we sin, if we repent sincerely, He covers our faults.

15 Power

الْقَهَّارُ

Al-Qahhar

The Subduer

The Dominant One who subdues all creation. Everything is humble and submissive before His power.

16 Generosity

الْوَهَّابُ

Al-Wahhab

The Bestower

The Giver who gives without expecting anything in return. His gifts are endless and not limited by our merit.

17 Provision

الرَّزَّاقُ

Ar-Razzaq

The Provider

The One who provides sustenance (physical and spiritual) to all creatures. He ensures no soul is left without provision.

18 Judgment

الْفَتَّاحُ

Al-Fattah

The Opener

The Judge who opens the doors of mercy and relief. He opens closed hearts to truth and solves difficult problems.

19 Knowledge

الْعَلِيمُ

Al-Alim

The All-Knowing

The Knower of all things, visible and invisible. Nothing is hidden from Him, not even the whisper of the soul.

20 Power

الْقَابِضُ

Al-Qabid

The Constrictor

The One who restricts provision or breathes difficulty as a test or protection. It is a form of divine care.

21 Mercy

الْبَاسِطُ

Al-Basit

The Expander

The One who expands provision and joy. He opens the chest of the believer to receive faith and tranquility.

22 Power

الْخَافِضُ

Al-Khafid

The Abaser

The One who lowers whom He wills due to their arrogance or disobedience. Humility before Him raises us.

23 Power

الرَّافِعُ

Ar-Rafi'

The Exalter

The One who raises whom He wills in status and honor. True elevation comes from piety and closeness to Him.

24 Honor

الْمُعِزُّ

Al-Mu'izz

The Honorer

The Bestower of Honor. He grants dignity and strength to those who obey Him and rely on Him.

25 Power

الْمُذِلُّ

Al-Mudhill

The Humiliator

The One who disgraces those who oppose Him. Disgrace comes from turning away from the Source of Honor.

26 Knowledge

السَّمِيعُ

As-Sami'

The All-Hearing

He hears every sound, word, and whisper. No prayer is too quiet for Him to hear and answer.

27 Knowledge

الْبَصِيرُ

Al-Basir

The All-Seeing

He sees everything, even what is hidden in darkness. His sight encompasses all time and space.

28 Justice

الْحَكَمُ

Al-Hakam

The Judge

The Ultimate Arbitrator whose judgment is final and just. On the Day of Judgment, only His verdict matters.

29 Justice

الْعَدْلُ

Al-Adl

The Just

The One who is perfectly just in all His actions. He never wrongs anyone, even by an atom's weight.

30 Kindness

اللَّطِيفُ

Al-Latif

The Subtle One

The One who is kind in hidden ways. He provides for us through means we cannot perceive or understand.

31 Knowledge

الْخَبِيرُ

Al-Khabir

The All-Aware

He knows the inner reality of things. He is aware of our intentions and the secrets of our hearts.

32 Patience

الْحَلِيمُ

Al-Halim

The Forbearing

He does not hasten to punish sinners. He gives us time to repent and return to Him.

33 Majesty

الْعَظِيمُ

Al-Azim

The Magnificent

The One whose greatness is beyond human comprehension. Our minds cannot fully grasp His magnitude.

34 Forgiveness

الْغَفُورُ

Al-Ghafur

The Forgiving

He forgives major sins for those who repent. His forgiveness wipes out the record of our mistakes.

35 Generosity

الشَّكُورُ

Ash-Shakur

The Appreciative

He rewards small deeds greatly. He appreciates the slightest effort we make towards goodness.

36 Majesty

الْعَلِيُّ

Al-Aliyy

The Most High

He is exalted above all creation. Nothing is higher than Him in status or essence.

37 Majesty

الْكَبِيرُ

Al-Kabir

The Greatest

The Majestic One who is greater than everything. All greatness belongs to Him alone.

38 Protection

الْحَفِيظُ

Al-Hafiz

The Preserver

He protects and preserves the heavens, the earth, and everything in them. He guards us from harm.

39 Provision

الْمُقِيتُ

Al-Muqit

The Sustainer

The Nourisher who provides both physical food and spiritual strength to sustain life.

40 Justice

الْحَسِيبُ

Al-Hasib

The Reckoner

The Sufficient One who takes account of all deeds. He is sufficient as a protector and helper.

41 Majesty

الْجَلِيلُ

Al-Jalil

The Majestic

The One of sublime majesty and bounty. He is revered and honored above all else.

42 Generosity

الْكَرِيمُ

Al-Karim

The Generous

The Bountiful One who gives generously without being asked. His generosity is limitless.

43 Knowledge

الرَّقِيبُ

Ar-Raqib

The Watchful

The Observer who watches over all things. He is always present and aware of our state.

44 Mercy

الْمُجِيبُ

Al-Mujib

The Responsive

The One who answers prayers. He responds to the call of the distressed and the needy.

45 Power

الْوَاسِعُ

Al-Wasi'

The All-Encompassing

His knowledge, mercy, and power encompass everything. Nothing is outside His reach.

46 Wisdom

الْحَكِيمُ

Al-Hakim

The Wise

The One who puts everything in its right place. His actions are full of wisdom and purpose.

47 Love

الْوَدُودُ

Al-Wadud

The Loving

The One who loves His believing servants and is loved by them. He is the source of all affection.

48 Majesty

الْمَجِيدُ

Al-Majid

The Glorious

The Noble and Glorious One. His essence and attributes are perfect and praiseworthy.

49 Power

الْبَاعِثُ

Al-Ba'ith

The Resurrector

The One who raises the dead for judgment. He also inspires souls to do good.

50 Knowledge

الشَّهِيدُ

Ash-Shahid

The Witness

He witnesses all things. Nothing happens in the universe without His knowledge and presence.

51 Truth

الْحَقُّ

The Truth

The Reality whose existence is undeniable. Everything else is temporary, but He is Eternal Truth.

52 Trust

الْوَكِيلُ

Al-Wakil

The Dependable One. We can entrust our affairs to Him, knowing He will handle them best.

53 Power

The Possessor of perfect strength. No fatigue or weakness touches Him.

54 Power

55 Protection

The Ally who supports and helps the believers. He is closer to us than our own selves.

56 Praise

Al-Hamid

He deserves all praise for who He is and what He does. All thanks ultimately return to Him.

57 Knowledge

Al-Muhsi

The Counter

He knows the number of all things. Nothing escapes His count, not even the leaves on a tree.

58 Creation

الْمُبْدِئُ

Al-Mubdi'

The Originator

59 Power

Al-Wajid

The Perceiver who finds whatever He seeks. He is never unaware or lacking anything.

65 Majesty

الْمَاجِدُ

Al-Majid

The Noble

The Illustrious and Beneficent. His nobility is reflected in His generous actions.

66 Oneness

Al-Wahid

The One

The Unique One who has no partner. He is indivisible and singular in His essence.

Al-Mu'akhkhir

Al-Barr

The Kind and Benevolent One. He is good to His creatures and accepts their good deeds.

At-Tawwab

The Acceptor of Repentance

He guides people to repent and then accepts their repentance. He loves those who turn to Him.

81 Justice

الْمُنْتَقِمُ

Al-Muntaqim

The Avenger

He punishes those who persist in evil and oppression. His justice requires accountability.

82 Forgiveness

الْعَفُوُّ

The King of all Kings. All dominion belongs to Him, and He gives power to whom He wills.

Lord of Majesty & Generosity

He combines perfect majesty with immense generosity. He is feared for His power and loved for His giving.

86 Justice

Al-Muqsit

The Equitable

The Fair One who judges with absolute fairness. He rights the wrongs and restores balance.

Al-Jami'

The Gatherer

88 Power

89 Provision

Al-Mughni

Al-Mani'

The Etiquette of Invocation (Adab al-Dua)

When calling upon Allah by His names, etiquette enhances the connection:

  • Praise First: Begin by praising Allah using names like Al-Hamid and Al-Karim.
  • Send Blessings: Send peace and blessings upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
  • Be Specific: Match the name to your need. Ask Al-Ghaffar for forgiveness and Ar-Razzaq for provision.
  • Certainty: Call upon Him with the certainty that He hears and will respond in the best way.

The Spiritual Impact of Knowing His Names

Deeply reflecting on the 99 Names transforms a believer's character. This process, known as Takhalluq bi Akhlaq Allah (adopting the character traits of Allah within human limits), means that if you know Allah is Ar-Rahim (Merciful), you strive to be merciful to others. If you know He is Al-Sabur (Patient), you cultivate patience. Thus, the Names are not just for recitation, but for transformation.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: "Allah has ninety-nine names, i.e., one-hundred minus one, and whoever knows them will go to Paradise."

— Sahih Bukhari
